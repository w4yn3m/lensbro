Questions

FAQ

What's your typical turnaround time?

For most projects, we deliver within 1-3 weeks post-shoot. Rush delivery is available for an additional fee. Social content like reels can be delivered within 48-72 hours.

Do you travel for shoots?

Absolutely! We're based in New Delhi but available for projects across India and internationally. Travel costs are discussed during project scoping.

What equipment do you use?

We shoot primarily on Cinema cameras (RED, Blackmagic), with Sony Alpha series for photography. Grading is done in DaVinci Resolve Studio.

How do I book a session?

Simply fill out the contact form above or email us directly. We'll schedule a discovery call to understand your vision, then provide a custom quote.

What's included in the deliverables?

You receive fully edited, color-graded files in your preferred formats. Raw footage, behind-the-scenes content, and multiple aspect ratios are available as add-ons.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, for larger projects we offer flexible payment schedules — typically 50% upfront and 50% on delivery. Custom arrangements can be discussed.
