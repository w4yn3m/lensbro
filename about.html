Our Story

About LensBro

Two Brothers,
One Vision

We are two brothers — founders of LensBro. What started as a shared obsession with capturing light and motion has evolved into a full-scale visual production agency.

We craft cinematic photography and editorial videography for brands, creators, and events. Our work balances bold composition, strong color, and narrative emotion.

Every project is a collaboration. We don't just capture moments — we create visual experiences that resonate, inspire, and leave lasting impressions.

The Journey

Our Timeline

2020

The Beginning

Started as two brothers with a camera, a laptop, and a dream. Shot first commercial for a local streetwear brand.

2021

First Major Project

Landed our first big brand deal. Shot a nationwide campaign for an emerging beverage brand. Revenue doubled.

2022

Studio Launch

Opened our first studio space. Invested in cinema-grade equipment. Started offering full post-production services.

2023

Award Season

Won multiple industry awards for best cinematography and color grading. Featured in national media outlets.

2024 — Present

Scaling Up

Expanded to international projects. Building a team of 8+ creatives. Working with top-tier brands and artists.
